Heming Regeneration

AI Large-Scale Guzheng Design · Systematic Approach

This project has developed an artificial intelligence large model dedicated to the personalized customization of the guzheng. Through intelligent algorithms, the model can generate innovative ancient and other design solutions that conform to the traditional cultural connotation according to the personalized needs and aesthetic preferences of users, aiming to ensure that the design not only retains the traditional cultural connotation, but also meets the diversified requirements of modern aesthetics. In order to cope with the limitations of existing designs.

BACKGROUND / RESEARCH CONTEXT

The Kitschification of the Guzheng

Due to the blend of entertainment and pan-entertainment trends, the guzheng has strayed from its traditional artistic values. Driven by hedonism and consumerism, it has become more of a commercial symbol rather than a carrier of culture, similar to the American news entertainment trend in the 1830s.

OBSERVATIONS FROM THE ORIGINAL PORTFOLIO
  • There are too many cheap guzheng on the market, sacrificing quality for low prices.
  • Vulgar paintings on guzheng cater to low-level aesthetics, lacking traditional charm.
  • Performers focus too much on showy effects, neglecting the essence of guzheng music.
  • Simplified scores and teaching content don’t represent the complexity of guzheng playing.
  • Consumerism overshadows the cultural inheritance of the guzheng.

Yueshan

The functions of Yueshan Timbre Influence: The article points out that the proportional relationship between the height of Yueshan and that of the bridges directly affects the intonation and timbre. 02 / RESEARCH

Strings

Silk strings: They produce a soft and pure sound, but the timbre is relatively dull. Steel strings: They have a crisp timbre and a loud volume, making them suitable for delicate musical pieces. Metal - nylon composite strings: They have excellent comprehensive characteristics and are suitable for pieces with rich harmonic effects.

03 / RESEARCH

Panel

Material and thickness Common Materials: The panel of a zheng is usually made of paulownia wood, which is favored for its moderate hardness and uniform texture. The Influence of Thickness: Too thick: The timbre is sharp and thin, and the volume is small. Too thin: The timbre is hollow and dry, lacking penetration. Appropriate Thickness: Selecting the right panel thickness is the key to ensuring the zheng produces a beautiful timbre.

04 / RESEARCH

Bottom Plate

Material selection Common Materials: The bottom plate usually uses the same material as the front panel, such as paulownia wood. Resonance Effect: Paulownia wood has excellent resonance properties, enhancing the fullness and pleasantness of the timbre. Thickness Design: The thickness of the bottom plate is generally 10mm. A flat design is maintained to facilitate sound transmission and resonance.

Sound holes

The size and placement of front, middle and rear sound holes affect resonance, aftersound and sound propagation.

Bridge material & placement

The shape, grain and positioning of hardwood bridges influence the transfer of string vibration and the quality of the timbre.

02 / LATENT SPACE

The Latent Space of the Guzheng Large Model

Innovation and exploration / original research description.

Model Building and Data

The latent space of the Guzheng AI generated image model is an innovative attempt of deep learning technology in the field of traditional art. It uses algorithms to deeply understand the aesthetic characteristics of the Guzheng, including the smoothness of lines, the harmony of colors, the delicacy of textures, etc., and transforms these abstract aesthetic concepts into computable vector representations.

The training of the model relies on a high-quality and diverse Guzheng image dataset. This includes Guzheng images from different eras, different regions, and different performance scenes, as well as the combination of Guzheng with different backgrounds and light and shadow effects. The richness and diversity of the dataset are the key to the model's ability to generate diverse images.

Exploration and Generation of Latent Space

Through deep learning algorithms, the model learns the common features and change patterns of Guzheng images from the dataset. This process involves multiple steps such as feature extraction, pattern recognition, and image generation, and finally forms a distribution in the latent space that can reflect the aesthetics of the Guzheng.

The latent space is a multidimensional vector space, and each dimension may correspond to a certain attribute or feature of the image. For example, one dimension may represent the saturation of the color, and another dimension may represent the thickness of the line. By adjusting the values of these dimensions, the style and details of the generated image can be controlled.

When the model receives a generation instruction, it randomly or purposefully selects a point in the latent space, which represents a specific set of image attributes. The model then maps this point back to the image space to generate a new guzheng image. This process can be controlled to generate images of specific styles or details.

Applications of Generated Images

By exploring and manipulating the latent space, researchers and artists can customize the style and details of the generated images, such as the style of the guzheng, the background environment, and the light and shadow effects. This can not only create unprecedented guzheng images, but also adjust them according to different needs and creativity, providing new perspectives and tools for the inheritance and innovation of guzheng culture.

By exploring the latent space, researchers can deeply understand the internal structure and aesthetic laws of guzheng images. This includes the composition of guzheng lines, the matching of colors, the use of light and shadow, etc., providing new data support and theoretical basis for the study of guzheng art.

Artists can use the images generated by the model as a source of inspiration to carry out innovative designs of guzheng art. Whether it is the appearance design of the guzheng or the creativity of the performance scene, the images generated by the model can provide unlimited creative space.

The high-quality guzheng images generated by the model can be used for the digital dissemination of guzheng culture, such as online exhibitions, digital publications, educational materials, etc. This will not only allow more people to appreciate the beauty of guzheng, but also promote international exchanges and cross-cultural dissemination of guzheng culture.

03 / EVALUATION

Modeling Evaluation & Feedback

Build preparation, model-space design and visual comparison.

Build Preparation

Dataset Construction

First, a large number of high-quality images related to Guzheng need to be collected, including Guzheng pictures of different styles, different backgrounds, and different lighting effects. The quality and diversity of the data set directly affect the generation effect and generalization ability of the model.

Model Architecture

Select or design a deep learning model suitable for generating images, such as generative adversarial networks (GANs), variational autoencoders (VAEs), or Transformer models with self-attention mechanisms. The model architecture needs to be able to effectively encode and decode image information while having the ability to generate new images.

Latent Space Design

In the model architecture, the design of the latent space is crucial. It is usually a multidimensional vector space, and each dimension represents a certain feature or attribute of the image. By adjusting the dimension and distribution of the latent space, the diversity and quality of the generated images can be controlled.

Training and Optimization

Use the data set to train the model, optimize the model parameters through methods such as backpropagation and gradient descent, so that the model can learn the generation rules of the image from the input data. During the training process, hyperparameters such as learning rate and batch size may need to be adjusted to achieve the best generation effect.

Evaluation and Adjustment

During and after the training, the generation effect of the model needs to be evaluated, including the clarity, realism, and diversity of the image. According to the evaluation results, the model architecture, training strategy or data set may need to be adjusted to optimize the generation effect.

Application and Innovation

After the model is built, it can be applied to multiple fields such as the generation of guzheng images, the innovative design of guzheng art, and the digital dissemination of guzheng culture, providing new possibilities for the inheritance and development of guzheng art.

04 / APPLICATIONS

Optimization and Utilization of Datasets

Innovative applications from a historical perspective.

Simulation and Innovation of Historical Styles

Using comprehensive historical image data, the model can learn the style characteristics of the guzheng in different historical periods, such as the gorgeousness of the Tang Dynasty, the exquisiteness of the Song Dynasty, and the simplicity of the Ming and Qing Dynasties. This can not only generate guzheng images with historical styles, but also make innovative designs based on historical styles, such as incorporating ancient decorative elements into modern guzheng designs.

Visualization of Historical Changes

The construction and optimization of the dataset provides a visualization tool for the historical changes of guzheng art. The images generated by the model can intuitively show the evolution of the guzheng from ancient times to the present, including changes in the body design, playing techniques, and musical styles, providing rich visual materials for the research and education of guzheng art.

A Bridge for Cross-cultural Dialogue

The cross-cultural and regional guzheng images in the dataset provide a bridge for cross-cultural dialogue and communication. The images generated by the model can show the commonalities and uniqueness of guzheng in different cultural backgrounds, promote global understanding and appreciation of guzheng art, and enhance mutual understanding and respect between different cultures.
